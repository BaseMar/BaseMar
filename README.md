### Habit Quest Analytics

An end-to-end productivity analytics application with RPG-inspired progression. It turns daily planning and check-ins into data users can use to understand their workload, consistency, and project progress.

[Repository](https://github.com/BaseMar/habit-quest-analytics) · [Live demo](https://habit-quest-analytics-sai5h3bl7wjykw4fbruabd.streamlit.app)

Choose **Try live demo** to explore a preloaded planner, record a check-in, and review the analytics. The guest workspace is isolated and resettable, and you can use it without signing in.

- **Planning and tracking** — Schedule tasks and recurring routines, break projects into work sessions, and record completion through daily check-ins.
- **KPIs and dashboards** — Compare performance with the previous period, track planned versus completed workload, and review category and routine performance, including current and best streaks.
- **Forecasting and progression** — Estimate project completion dates from the pace of completed work using rule-based forecasting. Earn XP based on planned duration when work is completed and progress through nonlinear character and stat levels.
- **Data modeling and testing** — PostgreSQL and SQLAlchemy model tasks, routines, projects, and check-ins. Alembic manages schema changes, a SQLite importer migrates legacy data, and pytest covers metrics, business rules, and persistence.
- **Authentication and deployment** — Google sign-in gives each account its own data. The app runs on Streamlit Community Cloud with Supabase PostgreSQL.

**Stack** · Python, SQL, PostgreSQL, SQLAlchemy, Alembic, Pandas, Plotly, Streamlit, Supabase, pytest.

### 🏋️ Workout Progress Analyzer

An end-to-end training analytics application that connects structured workout plans, recorded exercise performance, and explainable recommendations for future training sessions.**.

🔗 Repository: [ProgressAnalyzer](https://github.com/BaseMar/ProgressAnalyzer)  
🌐 Live demo: [Streamlit App](https://progressanalyzer-jysznkmwcjnejwamgmlfqd.streamlit.app)

Explore the **interactive demo** with generated training data, compare exercise performance, review training consistency, and preview recommendations for upcoming workouts without connecting to a personal database.

- **Training analytics** — Analyze exercise performance, repetition and load progression, workout consistency, muscle-group volume distribution, and body measurement trends.
- **Training plans and history** — Build weekly routines, configure training blocks and deloads, maintain versioned prescriptions, and link recorded workouts to planned sessions.
- **Recommendation engine** — Generate explainable, rule-based training targets using recorded exercise history, repetition ranges, working loads, RIR, and available equipment increments.
- **Data processing and modeling** — Import structured TXT workout notes, validate and normalize training records, and manage relational data with PostgreSQL, SQLite, and SQLAlchemy.
- **Testing and deployment** — Validate business rules, analytical calculations, database operations, and user workflows with automated tests and GitHub Actions. Support cloud deployment and local Docker environments.

**Stack** · Python, SQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite, SQLAlchemy, Pandas, Plotly, Streamlit, Supabase, pytest, Docker, GitHub Actions.
