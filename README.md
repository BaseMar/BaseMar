## 🚀 Featured Projects

### Habit Quest Analytics · Flagship Project

An end-to-end productivity analytics application with RPG-inspired progression. It turns daily planning and check-ins into data users can use to understand their workload, consistency, and project progress.

[Repository](https://github.com/BaseMar/habit-quest-analytics) · [Live demo](https://habit-quest-analytics-sai5h3bl7wjykw4fbruabd.streamlit.app)

Choose **Try live demo** to explore a preloaded planner, record a check-in, and review the analytics. The guest workspace is isolated and resettable, and you can use it without signing in.

- **Planning and tracking** — Schedule tasks and recurring routines, break projects into work sessions, and record completion through daily check-ins.
- **KPIs and dashboards** — Compare performance with the previous period, track planned versus completed workload, and review category and routine performance, including current and best streaks.
- **Forecasting and progression** — Estimate project completion dates from the pace of completed work using rule-based forecasting. Earn XP based on planned duration when work is completed and progress through nonlinear character and stat levels.
- **Data modeling and testing** — PostgreSQL and SQLAlchemy model tasks, routines, projects, and check-ins. Alembic manages schema changes, a SQLite importer migrates legacy data, and pytest covers metrics, business rules, and persistence.
- **Authentication and deployment** — Google sign-in gives each account its own data. The app runs on Streamlit Community Cloud with Supabase PostgreSQL.

**Stack** · Python, SQL, PostgreSQL, SQLAlchemy, Alembic, Pandas, Plotly, Streamlit, Supabase, pytest.
